APPLICANT CHECKLIST

Questions to Ask Before Joining

We do not publish fabricated testimonials. Please verify references and payment claims independently.

Who is the legal publisher?

Ask for the registered legal name, business address and verifiable registration details where applicable.

✓ Verify independently

Is any fee required?

Be cautious of registration, security, training, courier or account-activation fees demanded before work.

✓ Do not pay to unlock work

How is payment approved?

Get the acceptance rules, payment amount, due date and dispute process in writing before starting.

✓ Keep written records
Important: Work-from-home offers can be misrepresented. Do not share OTPs, banking passwords, full card details or identity documents in public chats. Verify the organisation and its claims before proceeding.
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to Common Inquiries

Review the basics about writing projects, payment and validation.

Is this opportunity genuine?

Please verify the publisher's identity, business details, references and written contract independently. This page alone is not proof of legitimacy.

How do I receive projects?

Use the Telegram link to request the current project brief. Confirm the scope, deadline and contact person before accepting.

How is payment made?

Payment method, amount, approval criteria and timing must be stated in a written agreement. Example figures on this page are not a guarantee.

Can students apply?

Eligibility depends on the specific project. Ask for the written eligibility requirements before applying.

Do I need a laptop?

Requirements depend on the assigned format. Confirm whether handwritten work, a computer or specific software is required.

Can I work part-time?

Ask the project coordinator about deadlines and expected daily workload. Flexibility is not guaranteed for every assignment.
